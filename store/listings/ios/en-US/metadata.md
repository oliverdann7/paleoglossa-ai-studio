## Description (4000 chars max)
Paleoglossa turns the dead languages into living reading.

Open any text in Ancient Greek, Koine Greek, Biblical Hebrew, Classical Latin, Syriac, Coptic, Aramaic, Akkadian, Sanskrit, Egyptian Hieroglyphs, or Hittite — and learn the way scholars actually read: one word at a time, with morphology, lemma, and translation a tap away.

KEY FEATURES
• Curated corpus — Iliad, Odyssey, Aeneid, the Hebrew Bible, the Greek New Testament (SBLGNT with morphology), Septuagint, Caesar, Cicero, Plato, the Bhagavad Gita, Gilgamesh, and dozens more.
• Word-by-word reader — tap any word for lemma, gloss, morphology, paradigm tables, and AI-generated philological notes.
• Spaced repetition — FSRS-5 algorithm schedules every word you mark for review at the optimal interval.
• AI philology tutor — six difficulty modes from beginner to scholar, conversation history, contextual quizzes.
• Grammar pathways — tiered progression through morphology, syntax, and rhetoric with prerequisite tracking.
• Pronunciation lab — restored, Erasmian, and modern reading traditions with audio playback.
• Research notebooks — capture cross-text notes with backlinks, export to Markdown or PDF.
• Imports — paste, file, URL, or OCR; supports PDF, EPUB, plain text, and TEI XML.
• Multi-device sync — Firebase-backed, picks up where you left off across web, iPad, and iPhone.
• Eight UI languages — English, Spanish, German, Portuguese, French, Russian, Chinese, Turkish.

SUBSCRIPTIONS
Subscription management is currently handled on the web at paleoglossa.com. Existing paid users retain full access on the iOS app after signing in.

ABOUT
Paleoglossa was built for serious readers of antiquity — divinity students, classics majors, semiticists, philologists, and lifelong learners who refuse to wait for a translation. Texts are drawn from public-domain and freely-licensed sources (SBLGNT, OSHB, Perseus, Project Gutenberg) with full attribution.
