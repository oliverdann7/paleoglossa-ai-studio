BeginnerGrammarSeminaryScholar
Why is μῆνιν accusative here, not nominative?
✦ Tutor
It is the direct object of the imperative ἄειδε ("sing!"). Homer opens with the object for emphasis — the poem is about the wrath.
μῆνις → acc. sg. μῆνιν. A 3rd-declension feminine i-stem.
