## Description (long-form, both stores)

Paleoglossa is a scholarly-grade reading platform for ancient languages.
Tap any word in Homer, the Greek New Testament, the Hebrew Bible,
Caesar, Gilgamesh, or the Bhagavad Gita — and instantly see lemma,
morphology, paradigm tables, and AI-powered translation.

KEY FEATURES
- 12 ancient languages: Ancient Greek, Koine Greek, Biblical Hebrew,
  Classical Latin, Syriac, Coptic, Aramaic, Akkadian, Sanskrit,
  Egyptian, Hittite, and Ugaritic.
- Immersive reader with knowledge states (NEW → LEARNING → KNOWN)
  that follow you across every text in the corpus.
- FSRS-5 spaced repetition built around the words you actually
  encountered while reading.
- AI philology tutor with six difficulty modes — from absolute
  beginner to graduate-level discussion.
- Full morphology engine with paradigm tables, dependency syntax
  trees (PROIEL / Gorman), and Logeion / Sefaria / Wiktionary
  lookups.
- Import your own PDFs, EPUBs, and pasted text. The app tokenizes,
  lemmatizes, and aligns the translation automatically.
- AudioLab: TTS for ancient languages with multiple pronunciation
  traditions (Restored Classical, Erasmian, Modern, Tiberian Hebrew).
- Research notebooks with cross-text backlinks; Markdown + PDF export.
- Grammar prerequisite graph with concept-level mastery tracking.
- Classroom mode for teachers and students.
- Cloud sync across web, iPad, iPhone, and Android. Works offline
  for previously viewed texts.

PRIVACY
- Email and account name are stored only to power your account.
- Audio recordings of your pronunciation stay private to you.
- No third-party advertising. Analytics are opt-out from Settings.

WEB-MANAGED SUBSCRIPTIONS
- Free tier includes the full corpus and unlimited reading.
- Premium features (custom imports, AI tutor extended quota) are
  managed at paleoglossa.com.

Visit paleoglossa.com for the privacy policy, terms, and support.
